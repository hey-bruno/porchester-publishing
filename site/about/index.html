Porchester Publishing

An independent imprint

Porchester Publishing is an independent imprint in London, founded in 2026. Its catalogue includes The Underqualified, standalone fiction and The Condensed Classics.

The Underqualified includes The Grand Galleria Detective Agency, Ltd., Stevens Creek Investigative Partners LLC and Little Venice Investigations & Boat Painting.

The standalone novels include horror and gothic mystery. The Condensed Classics presents substantial works in shorter editions, using the author’s prose or a classic English translation, with new illustrated reading guides.
