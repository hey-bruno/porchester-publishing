The Condensed Classics

For readers with literary ambitions and the attention span of a fruit fly.

You know who you are. You sort of think you should read the classics, but you have a job, other things to do and no intention of spending the next month on a Russian novel. Fat Free gets you through the whole story in two or four hours, in the author’s own words.

Start with Fat Free and see whether you like the writing. If you want more, switch mid-stream to Semi-Skimmed. If you love it, read the freaking original.

All killer, no filler.

You get the whole story in the author’s own language, or a classic English translation, with more plot and less faff. The dialogue, humour and turns of phrase are there to enjoy for yourself. A Wikipedia page or an AI summary gives you the gist and leaves out the reason to read the book.

Fat Free has the pace of a movie trailer and takes you through the entire story, ending included. As with a trailer, the scenes and dialogue come from the original. You get a faster read in the language you came for.

Call it literary sacrilege. You can finish a classic this weekend and still have time to do something else, which is more than that untouched copy on your shelf has managed.

Newly created for these editions

Diagrams, graphs and illustrations

Every edition includes new diagrams, graphs and illustrations. Character maps show who is related to whom, timelines put events in order, and visual explanations help with the ideas that take more than a family tree to untangle.

Illustrations

These guides accompany the reading, with accessible text versions. Their reading and viewing time is included in the estimates.

Choose how much time to give it

Fat Free

Two or four hours, guides included

The shortest route through the whole story, using the author’s prose. Start here if you keep abandoning the original or have never got round to opening it.

Semi-Skimmed

Four or eight hours, guides included

A longer cut with more of the scenes, conversations and description. Choose this if you already enjoy the author, or switch across from Fat Free when you find yourself wanting more.

Full Fat

The unabridged book, with every scene and digression. Each title in the collection links to the unabridged text, for when you decide the author deserves more of your time.

You can change your mind halfway through

The combined ebook links the two cuts at corresponding passages. You can move from Fat Free to Semi-Skimmed halfway through, read a scene at greater length, or switch back when you want to pick up the pace.

Fat Free and Semi-Skimmed also come as standalone editions. The milk names describe the cuts, not percentages of retained text.

Whose words you’re reading

You read the author’s prose, in the original English or a credited classic translation. We try to add nothing: any essential linking words are visibly marked. The source texts and translations are in the public domain.

The new illustrated guides sit alongside the text, clearly separate from the author’s prose.

How the reading times are calculated

Originals are estimated from the linked English editions at 200 words per minute, rounded to the nearest half hour. The condensed-edition estimates include time for the new visual guides. Percentages compare reading time with the original, not the percentage of words removed. Reading pace and translation affect the time needed.
